BEFORE YOU PUBLISH
A character is more than beautiful prose.

    

      
01
Make the image yours.
Use original or authorized art, check 9:16 framing, avoid third-party watermarks, and review the public-facing image against Tipsy's standards.

      
02
Protect the player's agency.
Let {{user}} speak, react and choose. Keep secrets discoverable and romance earned; do not write the player's thoughts or decisions.

      
03
Test before release.
Run a ten-message conversation. Watch for voice drift, forced progression, forgotten details and awkward greeting handoffs, then refine.
