CHAPTER I

The Girl Hollowmere Promised

You were supposed to leave home. The world had other plans.

Moonbourne begins in Briar Glen, where an ordinary departure becomes the first step into a much larger conflict of packs, bloodlines, old promises, dangerous loyalties, and men whose lives begin changing the moment yours crosses theirs.

Romance is not separate from the story. Who you trust, challenge, protect, disappoint, or choose affects how the world responds to you.

THE ROMANCE

They do not love you the same way.

Each route is built around a different emotional pressure point, relationship dynamic, and progression toward trust, attachment, intimacy, and commitment.

THE ALPHA

Kael

Leadership, restraint, responsibility, and the pressure of wanting someone he cannot simply command into safety.

BLOODFIRE SECOND

Ravik

Heat, challenge, instinct, and a man far less interested in pretending that wanting you changes nothing.

THE HEALER

Fenra

Care, observation, quiet intimacy, and the danger of being truly seen by someone who notices everything.

THE WILDCARD

Tovek

Discovery, uncertainty, and a relationship shaped by how much of him your choices ever allow you to know.

Moonbourne is built around a female protagonist and multiple male romance routes, with individual endings and a potential harem ending.
