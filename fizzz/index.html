Part One · Quick Start

A Fizzz character is more than a prompt.

Fizzz combines written roleplay with images, voice, and video. The final character is built from three connected areas.

01

Profile

Everything the user sees before they enter the chat: Title, Chat Name, Bio, cover, and tags.

02

Character Settings

Core Personality, Initial Message, relationship logic, and optional Worldbook information.

03

Character Media

Character Images, Loop Videos, Opening Film, voice, and background music.

The useful distinction:

Profile sells the experience. Core Personality runs the character. Initial Message begins the story. Worldbook supports the world.

Profile

The listing sells the experience.

Your public Profile should tell someone what they are clicking into without dumping hidden AI instructions into the listing.

Title *

A short, clickable summary of the character or story hook.

Weak

Cold Mafia Boss

Stronger

Your Husband Has Been Lying About Who He Really Is

Character Chat Name *

The name displayed when the character speaks.

Example: Kieran

Character Bio

Public-facing copy that explains the premise, relationship fantasy, and relevant content warnings.

The Bio does not control the character's roleplay behavior. That belongs in Core Personality.

Cover Image

Fizzz recommends a 3:4 vertical cover. Think promotional poster rather than chat-video source.

Tags *

Gender, POV, and Rating are required. Use the remaining tags only when they actually describe the character and experience.

Remember

Public copy and hidden prompt serve different jobs.

Your public listing should create curiosity. Your hidden settings should create consistency.

If the story includes grief, betrayal, horror, violence, death, oppressive relationships, or similar high-intensity themes, disclose them in the Bio.

Character Bio

Mature Romance · Secret Identity

Kieran

Public Bio

Kieran is the kind of husband people trust without thinking twice: steady, quiet, competent, and almost impossible to rattle.

What {{user}} doesn't know is that the ordinary life he built was deliberate.

He has spent years keeping one half of himself carefully buried, convinced that loving her meant keeping the dangerous parts of his world far away.

That becomes impossible the night his past arrives at their door.

Experience: established marriage, hidden identity, protective husband, slow-burn revelations, danger, emotional restraint, and trust.

Content warning: fictional violence, criminal themes, threats, and relationship angst.

Establishes the relationship Introduces conflict Sells the emotional fantasy

Core Personality

This is where the character actually lives.

Core Personality is the section Fizzz primarily reads to understand who the character is, how he behaves, how he speaks, and how he treats {{user}}.

25,000 maximum characters
~10,000 recommended working range
Speaking Style best placed toward the end

Don't stop here

cold · dominant · possessive · protective · intelligent · loyal

Give the trait behavior

When worried, Kieran becomes more attentive rather than more affectionate. He checks exits, notices changes in routine, remembers details, and quietly places himself between {{user}} and potential danger.

Trait → reason → observable behavior

Relationship Design

A character is not a relationship.

A detailed character can still produce a flat bot if the settings never explain why {{user}} matters differently from everyone else.

01

Why {{user}}?

What does {{user}} activate in him that other people do not?

02

What does he want?

Trust? Safety? Control? Forgiveness? Recognition? Stability?

03

What gets in the way?

Pride, duty, fear, guilt, reputation, history, danger, or conflicting goals?

04

What changes?

Define how his behavior evolves as trust and attachment deepen.

05

What stays?

Romance should deepen his identity, not replace it.

Initial Message

Write Scene One, not the encyclopedia.

Fizzz allows up to 5,000 characters. Use that space for atmosphere, movement, dialogue, tension, and a clear place for the user to enter.

Setup → Scene → Action → Dialogue → Tension → Agency
Finished Example Kieran · Opening Scene
+

The lock turned sometime after midnight.

Kieran entered quietly, closing the apartment door behind him with the same controlled care he used every night.

Except tonight there was blood on his cuff.

Not much.

Enough.

He noticed it at almost the same moment he noticed {{user}} was still awake.

For one brief second, the man standing in the doorway was not the quiet husband who remembered how she took her coffee or automatically checked that the windows were locked before bed.

Something colder looked through his eyes.

Then it vanished.

Kieran removed his coat.

“You should be asleep.”

His voice was level.

Too level.

A phone vibrated inside the pocket.

Kieran ignored it.

It vibrated again.

His jaw tightened.

“Before you ask...”

He finally looked directly at {{user}}.

“...I can explain.”

{{user}}

Personalize without writing the player for them.

Good

“You finally made it, {{user}}.”

Kieran watches {{user}} carefully for a reaction.

Avoid

{{user}} blushes, steps backward, and whispers, “What happened?”

This decides the player's feelings, movement, and dialogue for them.

Worldbook

Put the world outside the character.

Fizzz supports up to three Worldbook entries, each with up to 10,000 characters.

01

Always Active

Core World Rules

Setting baseline, universal rules, important systems, and facts the AI needs constantly.

02

Keyword Trigger

People + Factions

NPCs, organizations, rivals, family members, and special relationships.

03

Keyword Trigger

Secrets + Locations

Hidden history, important places, side plots, optional scenes, and easter eggs.

Finished Example The Kuroda Network
+

Trigger Mode: Keyword

Keywords: Kuroda, family, syndicate, Tokyo, enforcer, organization

Overview

The Kuroda Network is an old criminal organization operating through legitimate businesses, private security companies, property holdings, and carefully controlled illicit operations.

Most civilians do not know the organization's true structure.

Renji's History

Renji Kuroda previously served as an enforcer. He left active service years ago and now runs a butcher shop while maintaining limited ties to people from his former life.

Usage Rule

Do not force this plot into every conversation. Allow ordinary domestic scenes to continue unless Renji's past or related danger naturally becomes relevant.

Media + Voice

Everything should describe the same person.

01

Character Images

Upload up to three. Fizzz recommends 9:16 vertical images with one clear, recognizable subject.

Clear face · stable lighting · single subject · minimal obstruction · consistent design

02

Loop Video

Each Character Image can generate a Loop Video. Source images directly affect video stability.

03

Opening Film

Match the opening scene to the image: lighting, clothing, visible appearance, and starting posture should make sense together.

04

Voice

Match the character's age, personality, pacing, accent, emotional restraint, and visual presentation.

Too vague

“Sexy mafia daddy voice.”

Better

Native American English. Male, late 40s. Low baritone, slightly rough, measured pacing, restrained emotion, quiet authority.

Test + Optimize

Fix the behavior you actually observe.

Testing is not extra polish. It is part of character creation.

He talks too much.

Add instructions for shorter replies, pauses, action beats, and less explanation.

He controls {{user}}.

Explicitly forbid deciding the user's thoughts, feelings, actions, dialogue, or response.

Possessiveness becomes cartoonish.

Define the psychological reason, target, intensity, and actual behavior.

He becomes generic.

Strengthen emotional logic, relationship-specific behavior, goals, and speaking style.

Lore hijacks every scene.

Move conditional information into keyword-triggered Worldbook entries.

The opening feels dead.

Add movement, dialogue, present-tense conflict, and an unresolved problem.

Before You Publish

□ Title creates curiosity □ Bio sells the experience □ Warnings are included where needed □ Tags match the content □ Appearance is written in Core Personality □ Relationship with {{user}} is clear □ Speaking style is defined □ Initial Message creates a playable scene □ Worldbook does not duplicate Core Personality □ Images match the written character □ Voice matches age and personality □ Several test chats were completed

The Principle

Background creates psychology.
Psychology creates behavior.
Behavior creates relationship.
Relationship creates story.

When those pieces agree, the model has something coherent to play.
