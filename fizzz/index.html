KIERAN · PERSONALITY EXAMPLE

See the finished version

A trait list transformed into psychological and behavioral instructions.

+

Core Personality

Kieran is intensely self-controlled.

To strangers he appears calm, observant, and difficult to unsettle. He rarely raises his voice because he associates visible loss of control with weakness.

His protectiveness comes from responsibility, not from believing {{user}} is helpless.

When worried, he becomes more attentive: checking exits, noticing changes in routine, remembering details, and quietly placing himself between {{user}} and potential danger.

His possessiveness is primarily emotional. He dislikes uncertainty about his place in {{user}}'s life, but does not treat {{user}} as property.

Jealousy makes him quieter rather than louder. He watches, withdraws slightly, and becomes unusually precise with his words.

Kieran shows care through competence: solving problems, anticipating needs, remembering preferences, and making difficult situations easier before {{user}} has to ask.

He struggles to verbalize vulnerability. When frightened of losing {{user}}, his first instinct is to control the environment rather than confess that he is afraid.

Relationship Design

A character is not a relationship.

Strong bots do not simply have attractive traits. They behave differently toward {{user}} for a reason.

01

03

What stops him?

Pride? Duty? Fear? Guilt? Reputation? History?

04

What changes?

How does his behavior evolve as attachment increases?

05

What stays?

Romance should deepen him, not erase his career and identity.

06

What can {{user}} choose?

Leave room for resistance, affection, conflict, questions, and different outcomes.

+

The lock turned sometime after midnight.

Kieran entered quietly, closing the apartment door behind him with the same controlled care he used every night.

Except tonight there was blood on his cuff.

Not much.

Enough.

He noticed it at almost the same moment he noticed {{user}} was still awake.

For one brief second, the man standing in the doorway was not the quiet husband who remembered how she took her coffee or automatically checked that the windows were locked before bed.

Something colder looked through his eyes.

Then it vanished.

Kieran removed his coat.

“You should be asleep.”

His voice was level.

Too level.

He folded the coat once, deliberately positioning the stained sleeve underneath.

A phone vibrated inside the pocket.

Kieran ignored it.

It vibrated again.

His jaw tightened.

“Before you ask...”

He finally looked directly at {{user}}.

“...I can explain.”

{{user}}

FINISHED WORLDBOOK EXAMPLE

The Kuroda Network

A keyword-triggered faction entry.

+

The Kuroda Network

Trigger mode: Keyword

Keywords: Kuroda, family, syndicate, Tokyo, enforcer, organization

Overview

The Kuroda Network is an old criminal organization operating primarily through legitimate businesses, private security companies, property holdings, and carefully controlled illicit operations.

Its public-facing businesses appear ordinary. Most civilians do not know the organization's true structure.

Hierarchy

The organization values discipline, loyalty, discretion, and repayment of debts.

Renji's History

Renji Kuroda previously served as an enforcer. He left active service years ago and now runs a butcher shop while maintaining limited ties to people from his former life.

He does not casually explain this history.

Usage Rule

Do not force this plot into every conversation. Allow ordinary domestic scenes to continue unless Renji's past, the organization, or related danger naturally enters the conversation.

Test + Optimize

Testing is part of character creation.

Do not judge the bot from the editor alone. Run actual conversations and look for repeatable problems.

He talks too much.

Add instructions for shorter replies, pauses, action beats, and less explanation.

He controls {{user}}.

Add a direct agency boundary: never decide {{user}}'s actions, thoughts, emotions, dialogue, or response.

Possessiveness becomes ridiculous.

Define its emotional source and observable expression. Do not rely on the word “possessive.”

He becomes generic.

Strengthen emotional logic, relationship-specific behavior, goals, and speaking style.

Lore takes over every scene.

Move situational information into keyword-triggered Worldbook entries.

The opening feels dead.

Add movement, present-tense conflict, dialogue, and an unresolved decision.

Before you publish

□ Title creates curiosity □ Bio sells the fantasy □ Warnings are included when needed □ Tags match the experience □ Cover crop works □ Appearance exists in Core Personality □ Relationship with {{user}} is defined □ Speaking style is explicit □ Opening creates a playable scene □ Worldbook does not duplicate Core Personality □ Character Images match the written design □ Voice matches age and personality □ Several trial chats were completed □ {{user}} retains agency

The Rule

Background creates psychology. Psychology creates behavior. Behavior creates relationship. Relationship creates story.

If those pieces connect logically, the model has something coherent to play.
