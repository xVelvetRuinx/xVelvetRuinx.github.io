DISCOVERY TAGS · SEVEN OPTIONAL + THREE REQUIRED

Fizzz requires a Gender, POV and Rating choice. Fizzz allows up to ten tags total including the required Gender, POV, and Rating categories. That leaves at most seven additional tags here; confirm exact options when publishing.

CHAPTER TWO / AI-READ CHARACTER SETTINGS

The person behind the premise.

Fizzz permits up to 25,000 characters in Core Personality and recommends staying within 10,000. Build identity, causal psychology, consistent behavior and speaking style. You can use more when complexity genuinely requires it.

CHAPTER THREE / FIRST IMPRESSION
Open a door. Don't finish the story.
Up to 5,000 characters. A strong opening gives players a scene, visible movement, a natural spoken line for the Opening Film, and something to respond to—without dictating {{user}}'s feelings or actions.

CHAPTER FOUR / OPTIONAL WORLDBOOK
Give the world its own memory.
Fizzz allows up to three entries, each up to 10,000 characters. Use Always Active for indispensable rules and keyword-triggered entries for specific NPCs, locations, secrets and scenes.




✧ Keyword trigger advice
Use natural, distinctive words players or characters might actually say. Avoid generic terms that fire every turn. A keyword does not automatically reveal a secret; write what the character knows and when they should share it.








CHAPTER FIVE / CHARACTER MEDIA
Make every element the same person.
Fizzz is multimodal: text, images, voice and video must reinforce one another. This studio prepares a media brief and checklist; actual assets are uploaded in Fizzz.


OFFICIAL IMAGE SHAPES
Cover: recommended 3:4, face visible. Character Images: up to three, recommended 9:16, recognizable single subject, stable lighting. Voice: required before Opening Film; Design Voice ≤20 words. Supplied guide quotas: 12 free AI Loop Video generations/day, 10 free Opening Film generations/day (confirm the current in-app limit). Background Music is optional.

CHAPTER SIX / FINAL MANUSCRIPT
Ready to become real?
Get platform-aware feedback, export each field, or back up the full draft. Final publishing and uploading happen on Fizzz.


✧ The last pass is about performance, not deleting personality.
Simple familiar hooks often attract the first click; specific reactions, growth and story consequences sustain the chat. If the core is wordy, review exact redundancies—not meaningful lore or dialogue.
