CHAPTER FOUR / OPTIONAL WORLDBOOK
Give the world its own memory.
Fizzz allows up to three entries, each up to 10,000 characters. Use Always Active for indispensable rules and keyword-triggered entries for specific NPCs, locations, secrets and scenes.




✧ Keyword trigger advice
Use natural, distinctive words players or characters might actually say. Avoid generic terms that fire every turn. A keyword does not automatically reveal a secret; write what the character knows and when they should share it.








CHAPTER FIVE / CHARACTER MEDIA
Make every element the same person.
Fizzz is multimodal: text, images, voice and video must reinforce one another. This studio prepares a media brief and checklist; actual assets are uploaded in Fizzz.


OFFICIAL IMAGE SHAPES
Cover: recommended 3:4, face visible. Character Images: up to three, recommended 9:16, recognizable single subject, stable lighting. Voice: required. Opening Film and Loop Videos are created in Fizzz.
