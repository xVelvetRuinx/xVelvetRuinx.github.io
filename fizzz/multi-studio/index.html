THE CORRECT FIZZZ WORKFLOW

Individual bots first. Multi-bot second.

  1. Create each character separately. Give Miles, Wren, Sebastian, Genevieve and Elias their own standalone profiles, personality, initial message, portrait and individual lore. Save each in your library.
  2. Export each one. Download the individual Character TXT, Worldbooks TXT and portrait. Worldbooks are NOT embedded in the Character TXT or card.
  3. Create/upload those five characters inside Fizzz. Enter each field into the corresponding standalone bot. Publish or save as Fizzz requires; then record its URL/ID back in this studio.
  4. Create the Multi-Character bot on Fizzz. In Fizzz's multibot builder, choose the already-created characters from the list, instead of trying to upload a single combined character card.
  5. Transfer the group scenario. Use the group's title, bio, opening, shared Worldbooks, and interaction guidance. Follow the fields Fizzz actually offers. Test entrances, speaking order, continuity and {{user}} autonomy.
